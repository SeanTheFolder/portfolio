The problems I’m brought in to solve.

Cyber insurance

“Our renewal is in ninety days and the questionnaire asks for controls we’re not sure we have.”

I treat the renewal application as a structured external assessment. Map every question to a real control, close the gaps that move the premium, and turn the renewal into a board-ready posture conversation instead of a fire drill.

OCR / HIPAA audit

“An OCR audit notice just arrived and we have ten business days to respond.”

I assemble the response against what OCR actually asks for: a current risk analysis, the evidence that each policy operates, and the binder organized so the response is a filing exercise instead of a three-week scramble that still comes up short.

Program build

“We have security tools but no program. Alerts nobody triages, policies nobody follows.”

I build the program the tools were supposed to serve: a CIS Controls v8 baseline crosswalked to HIPAA, sequenced over six months, on a budget you can defend. Inventory and identity first, detection that produces signal, and an audit on the calendar as the forcing function.

AI governance

04 / How an engagement works

No surprises.

The same five steps whether it is a two-week assessment or a six-month build. You always know what is happening, what it costs, and what you get.

  1. Scoping call

    Thirty minutes, free, no pitch. We confirm the real problem, the constraints, and whether I am the right person. If I am not, I say so and point you somewhere better.

  2. Assessment

    I look at the actual environment, not the org chart’s version of it. You get written findings in business terms: operational continuity, regulatory exposure, reputational cost.

  3. Roadmap

    A prioritized plan with owners, dates, and a budget envelope. We align before anything gets built. No work starts on assumptions.

  4. Execution

    Hands-on delivery with visible milestones. You see progress on a cadence we agree to up front, reported the way your board already reads.

  5. Clean handoff

    Documentation, runbooks, and knowledge transfer so your team owns it. The goal is to make myself unnecessary, not indispensable.

06 / Fit

Whether I’m the right call.

Strong fit

  • Healthcare, home health, hospice, behavioral health, or another HIPAA-regulated environment.
  • You need a builder who is hands-on and can present risk to a board, not one or the other.
  • A real deadline is driving this: a renewal, an audit, a deal, a deployment, a vacancy.
  • You want the engagement to make your team stronger, not create a permanent dependency.
  • Lean budget, high stakes, and a need for enterprise-grade posture without enterprise spend.

Probably not the right call

  • You want a name on a compliance document and no actual change to how things run.
  • You need a large dedicated team on site full-time from day one across multiple workstreams.
  • You are looking for a speaker, panelist, or podcast guest. I contribute in writing, not on stage.
  • The goal is to check a box for a customer rather than to genuinely reduce risk.
  • You want the cheapest possible vendor and outcomes are negotiable.
