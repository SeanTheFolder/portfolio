04 / Principles

Security is not a cost center. It’s a trust engine.

The full philosophy
No. 1

Security enables the mission

In healthcare, security makes “yes” possible safely. Every control protects the ability to serve patients.

No. 2

Translation is the job

Converting CVEs into funded decisions is the work. Every significant risk maps to operational continuity, regulatory exposure, or reputational cost.

No. 3

Models are rented, governance is owned

Single-vendor LLM lock-in is the strategic mistake of 2026. Govern the trunk so any model can pass through it.
