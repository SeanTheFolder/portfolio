07 / How it works

The request mechanics

  1. Pick a pack

    Click the request link. Your mail client opens with a prefilled subject and a short body asking for the context details that let me tailor the materials to your situation.

  2. I reply by hand

    Usually the same business day, always within twenty-four hours during the working week. The reply is a direct email with the templates attached. No signup, no mailing list, no marketing automation, no autoresponder.

  3. The packs are working artifacts

    Each one was used in real engagements, not reverse-engineered from the essay. They calibrate to your size band and maturity level when you mention those in the request. Use them, modify them, attribute or do not. They are sent to be useful.

  4. If a pack sparks an engagement question

    Mention it in the request and we can set up a thirty-minute exploratory call by reply. No obligation, no sales motion.
