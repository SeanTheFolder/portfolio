How the request works

Pick a pack. Click the request button. Your mail client opens with a prefilled subject and a short body asking for the context details that let me tailor the materials to your situation.

I reply by hand. Usually the same business day, always within twenty-four hours during the working week. The reply is a direct email with the templates attached — no signup, no mailing list, no marketing automation, no autoresponder.

The packs are working artifacts. Each one was used in real engagements, not reverse-engineered from the essay. They calibrate to your size band and maturity level when you mention those in the request. Use them, modify them, attribute or do not — they are sent to be useful.

If a pack sparks an actual engagement question, mention it in the request and we can set up a thirty-minute exploratory call by reply. No obligation, no sales motion.
