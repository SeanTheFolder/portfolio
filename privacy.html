Privacy

A CISO’s website should practice what it preaches.

Plain-English privacy practices for this site: what is collected, how it is used, how it is protected, and how to exercise your rights. Last updated: April 2026.

What happens when you visit

This site is a personal portfolio and consulting brochure. It does not handle protected health information (PHI), payment information, or any data covered by HIPAA. The practices below reflect that scope. There are no advertising networks, no behavioral tracking, no third-party tracking cookies, and no data broker integrations. That is by design.

What this site collects

Information you provide

When you send an email, the following is collected and stored: your name, your company (if you include it), your email address, and the content of your message. This is used solely to respond to your inquiry. It is not sold, traded, or used for unrelated marketing.

Information collected automatically

Standard server logs may capture IP address, browser type, referring page, and the time of visit. This is used for operational purposes such as diagnosing issues and understanding traffic patterns, and is retained for 30 days. Aggregated, non-identifying analytics may be collected to understand which pages are useful.

Local storage

Your color-theme preference (light or dark) is stored in your browser’s local storage. It never leaves your device.

What is not collected

No health information, payment information, social security numbers, or sensitive personal data of any kind.

Use, sharing, and retention

Use. Inquiry emails are used to respond to you and to discuss the engagement you described. They may be retained in the email and CRM systems used by the practice for ongoing client relationship management.

Sharing. Information you provide is not shared with third parties for marketing. Vendors that process information on behalf of this site (email provider, CRM provider, hosting provider) operate under standard data processing agreements with appropriate safeguards.

Retention. Server logs are kept for 30 days. Inquiry communications are retained for as long as necessary to maintain the professional relationship, or as required by professional standards. You may request deletion at any time.

Your rights

You can ask what information is held about you, request a copy, request correction, request deletion, or opt out of any future communication. Email sean.welding@email.com with the subject line Privacy Request and indicate what you would like to do.

Security

HTTPS-only, standards-compliant, and intentionally minimal. The less collected, the less there is to protect. Vulnerability reports are welcome via security.txt.
