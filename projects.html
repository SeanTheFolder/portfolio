Index of Work

Every engagement, documented.

35 engagements across a decade: security programs built from zero, compliance under live deadlines, AI governance in a HIPAA boundary, and the infrastructure underneath all of it. Each one written up as a case study — problem, approach, outcomes, lessons.

  1. 01 Real-Time Staffing & Census Forecasting Model Built a department-by-department staffing and census forecasting model that updates in near real time and projects 30-, 60-, and 90-day demand. Replaces a monthly spreadsheet review cycle with a live model that the COO, CFO, and clinical directors actually use to make hire-or-hold decisions in the same week the trend appears. 2026 · Healthcare IT · ongoing
  2. 02 Executive Operating Dashboard for Board & C-Suite Designed and shipped the first true executive operating dashboard the organization has ever had. One screen, four quadrants, every leading indicator the board and C-suite need to govern the organization. 2026 · Operations · ongoing

Department heads stopped emailing IT for ad-hoc reports and started running their own weekly operating reviews against the same numbers leadership is reading. 2026 · Operations · ongoing
  4. 04 Multi-LLM Evaluation Framework for Healthcare Use Cases Structured evaluation framework comparing Claude, Gemini, GPT, Antigravity, and Microsoft Copilot across the actual tasks a healthcare IT and security team faces. Pick the right model for the right task instead of defaulting to one vendor. 2026 · AI & Automation · ongoing
  5. 05 Copilot Studio AI Agents for Security & Operations Custom AI agents in Copilot Studio for service desk triage, security alert classification, QA automation, and policy lookup. Each agent operates under HIPAA-aligned governance: Purview DLP on prompts, Conditional Access on agent surfaces, full audit logging through Sentinel. 2026 · AI & Automation · ongoing
  6. 06 42 CFR Part 2 Compliance Program for SUD Records Built and shipped the organization's 42 CFR Part 2 compliance program ahead of the February 2026 enforcement deadline. Covers SUD record confidentiality, consent management, segregation of regulated records, and breach notification obligations newly aligned with HITECH. 2026 · Compliance
  7. 07 Claude Code & MCP Workflow Integration for IT and Security Integrated Claude Code into the daily IT and security workflow at Harbors.   9. 09 Paperclip: Open-Source AI Company Emulator Open-source multi-agent simulation that emulates how an AI-driven company operates. Different LLM-backed agents play roles like CEO, engineer, analyst, and operator. 2025 · AI & Automation · ongoing

Cut annual spend by a meaningful percentage, decommissioned a long tail of unused tools, and stood up a quarterly review process so the spend never re-bloats. 2025 · Operations
  11. 11 Continuous Improvement Program & Idea Pipeline Stood up a structured continuous-improvement program from scratch. Any employee can submit an operational improvement idea in under two minutes, every idea is triaged transparently against the same rubric, and the implementation status is visible to the person who submitted it through to delivery. 2025 · Operations
  12. 12 Microsoft Purview DLP: Full Enterprise Deployment Microsoft Purview DLP across every M365 workload: Teams, Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive, and endpoint. PHI pattern detection blocks exfiltration at the edge while routing alerts into Sentinel for unified incident response. 2025 · Compliance

Eliminated legacy authentication, hardened service-account boundaries, cleared the cyber insurance identity-control gate, and shipped the rollout without a single after-hours clinical disruption. 2024 · Security
  26. 26 Autopilot & Intune: Full Endpoint Standardization Standardized the entire device fleet on Microsoft Autopilot and Intune. New hardware ships zero-touch from the OEM. 2024 · Infrastructure

  28. 28 Microsoft Intune Migration from MaaS360 Migrated the entire device fleet off MaaS360 onto Microsoft Intune in 90 days with zero downtime and 100% enrollment.
