ADP Workforce Now: First HRIS from Nothing

Implemented the organization's first HRIS. ADP Workforce Now replaced paper HR files and manual payroll. Data migrated, benefits onboarded, time and attendance digitized, ACA compliance automated, and the system wired into Entra ID for identity lifecycle.

Date
March 2024
Duration
14 weeks
Scope
Enterprise
Status
Completed

01 / The problem

HR ran on paper. Payroll was manually calculated and submitted. ACA compliance was a quarterly scramble assembled out of spreadsheets. Onboarding a new hire meant a folder, a clipboard, and a series of emails to provision systems. There was no system of record for employees.

02 / The approach

  1. Cleaned and migrated employee, benefits, and pay-rate data from paper and spreadsheets into a structured HRIS schema
  2. Stood up benefits administration including open enrollment workflows and ACA tracking against the 1095-C reporting requirement
  3. Configured time and attendance with manager approval workflows and exceptions handling for the clinical workforce
  4. Wired ADP into Entra ID so a hire in HR provisions a user in identity, and a termination revokes access. The IT lifecycle is now a closed loop
  5. Trained HR, payroll, and managers on the new flows and decommissioned the paper processes

04 / What I learned

HRIS migrations succeed or fail in the data cleanup that happens before go-live, not the configuration. Bad data on paper looks tolerable. Bad data in a system that runs payroll causes real-world checks to be wrong. Spend the time scrubbing names, dates, employment classifications, and benefits elections before the first import. Also: HRIS-to-IDP integration is the right place to close the offboarding loop. An HR termination should not require a separate IT ticket to revoke access.
