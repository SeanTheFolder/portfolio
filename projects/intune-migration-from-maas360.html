Microsoft Intune Migration from MaaS360

Migrated the entire device fleet off MaaS360 onto Microsoft Intune in 90 days with zero downtime and 100% enrollment. Eliminated a redundant MDM license stack while bringing endpoint compliance under the same identity and Conditional Access controls as everything else.

Date
April 2024
Duration
90 days
Scope
Enterprise
Status
Completed

01 / The problem

MaaS360 was a parallel MDM living outside the Microsoft 365 identity stack. It cost real money in licensing, required separate compliance reporting, and created a gap where Conditional Access couldn't enforce against device posture from another vendor. Two MDMs is one MDM too many.

02 / The approach

  1. Built compliance and configuration policies in Intune that matched or exceeded the existing MaaS360 baselines before any device moved
  2. Configured Autopilot for zero-touch provisioning so future enrollments required no manual MDM enrollment steps
  3. Migrated devices in waves by department, validating compliance posture before retiring the MaaS360 enrollment
  4. Wired Conditional Access policies to enforce Intune compliance status across all M365 access. Non-compliant devices are blocked
  5. Closed out the MaaS360 contract on schedule, eliminating the duplicate licensing line item

04 / What I learned

MDM migrations succeed or fail in the policy parity work that happens before any device moves. If your replacement compliance baseline doesn't already match what you're migrating off of, you'll spend the migration window debugging why devices fall out of compliance instead of moving them. Spend two weeks in policy authoring before you touch the first device, then the migration itself is mechanical.
